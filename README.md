# Data-Analytics
HR Dashboard in Excel

1. General Overview (Top Section)
   - Total Employees (Total Er): Displays a summary of active employees, categorized by gender, employment type (FT/PT), and compensation type (hourly/salary).
   - **Turnover Rate:** Shows employee turnover percentages for the years 2015-2018.
   - **Age Distribution:** Illustrates the distribution of employees by age group (<30, 30-49, 50+) and gender.

---

2. **Filters (Left Sidebar)**
   - **Full-Time/Part-Time (FT/PT):** Filter to view metrics for either employment type.
   - **Gender (F/M):** Filter to isolate metrics by gender.
   - **Region:** Filters employees by regions such as Central, East, Midwest, etc.
   - **Ethnic Group:** Filters employees by predefined ethnic group categories (Group A-G).

---

3. **Charts and Insights (Main Dashboard)**
   - **Total Active Employees (Top Center):** A bar chart showing the trend of active employees over time, with new hires highlighted.
   - **Separations (Bottom Left):** A bar chart tracking separations and "bad hires" over the years (2015-2018).
   - **Termination Reasons (Bottom Left):** Breaks down separations into "voluntary" and "involuntary" categories.
   - **Active Employees by Region (Middle Right):** A bar chart comparing active FT and PT employees by region.
   - **Active by Ethnic Group (Top Right):** Analyzes employees by ethnic group and employment type (FT/PT).
   - **Average Tenure in Months (Bottom Right):** Displays average tenure in months across ethnic groups and genders.
